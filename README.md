# Blessings — Start Counting What You Have 🌙

Feeling sad, anxious, overwhelmed, or going through a difficult time?

Sometimes, we become so focused on what we lack that we forget about the countless blessings Allah has already given us.

A home to sleep in, food on our table, clean water, family, health, eyesight, and even the opportunity to wake up to another day.

We often take these things for granted, while others are making du'a for exactly what we already have.

**Start counting your blessings. Appreciate what you have. Always be grateful.**

## What is Blessings?

Blessings is an interactive Islamic gratitude website designed to help you recognize, appreciate, and reflect on the blessings in your everyday life.

The website offers two main sections:

### 1. Recognize Your Blessings
Explore hundreds of everyday blessings through interactive cards and thoughtful reminders. Take a moment to recognize things you might normally overlook and understand how valuable they truly are.

### 2. Alhamdulillah
An interactive experience where you discover different blessings through draggable cards. Read each reminder, reflect on what Allah has provided, and move the cards as you acknowledge the blessings in your life.

## Our Purpose

This website is not about comparing your life with others or pretending that difficulties don't exist.

It's about changing your perspective, developing gratitude, and remembering Allah even during difficult moments.

You don't have to own everything to have a life full of blessings.

**"If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." — Quran 14:7**

Take a moment. Look around. Count your blessings.

**Alhamdulillah for everything. 🤍**
